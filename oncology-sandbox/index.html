The mechanism

IR = L · E · V
R = IR · (1 − ρ)  — bounded 0–1
EAL = L · C · (1 − ρ)
E_log(N) = [log₁₀N − log₁₀N_min] / [log₁₀N_max − log₁₀N_min]
Resilience enters as (1−ρ), never ρ directly, so coping capacity always lowers risk.

Before you use this tool

This is a research and teaching prototype. Please read what it is, and what it is not.

  • What it does. It applies the Risk Mechanism Theory Index (RMTI) to hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and other surveillance settings. It turns the numbers you enter into a relative, rubric-based score, a tier and a “framework reading”. It shows its equations and every input so the logic can be checked.
  • What it is not. It is not a medical device and has not been reviewed, cleared or approved by the FDA or any other regulator. It has not been clinically validated, and the HCC application is unpublished work in preparation that has not completed peer review. Its scores are not probabilities that a person will develop cancer. It does not diagnose, stage, predict outcomes or prescribe treatment.
  • How to read the messages on screen. Statements about surveillance intensity, work-up or referral are readings of the research framework, offered for discussion by a clinical team. They are not recommendations for any patient.
  • Who decides. Decisions about a real patient rest with the treating clinicians, using current clinical guidelines, complete clinical information and their own professional judgment. The software is provided “as is”, without warranty (Apache License, Version 2.0).
  • Your data. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Please do not type patient names or other identifying details.
